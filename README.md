Welcome to "Evade & Shun: Bacterial"!

This is a never before seen Infection mod which adds a deadly, fast spreading infection to your world.

You must FIGHT to defened against the onslaught of bacteria and eventually strike back!

In this mod, be careful about everything! Watch out for the outbreaks!

You might think your base is safe... but then you realize... no where is safe!

Use the tools at your disposal (Hazmat suit & Sanitizer) and attempt to push them back!

This mod comes with, but is not limited to: Horrors beyond comprehension! Monster that fight ANYTHING! Situaions that make you wish you had a nuclear tech mod! Jerry! and LOTS more!!!

Hope you know how to learn, because this mod doesn't serve anything on a silver platter!

Good luck out there! Try to stay in one piece!
